# L4-006 Phase 3 handoff

Official FRED `FYFSGDA188S` annual federal surplus/deficit as a percent of GDP
is passed through as a deterministic percentage-of-GDP value. FRED’s sign is
preserved: negative means deficit and positive means surplus. The handoff
retains annual observation dates, availability status, validation flag, and
the source manifest reference; no sign inversion or synthetic observation is
introduced.
