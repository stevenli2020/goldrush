# L9-004 — India physical gold imports and consumer demand

The parser reads the shared WGC GDT workbook's canonical India rows from `Jewellery`, `Bar and Coin`, and `India Supply`. It emits separate annual and quarterly metric-tonne components for jewellery demand, bar-and-coin demand, gross bullion imports, and net bullion imports. `observation_period_type` is `annual` or `quarterly`; annual totals and Q4 flows legitimately share December 31 and remain distinct records through the `(component, observation_period_type, observation_period)` identity. No combined demand or import inference is made. `India Supply` is a WGC/Metals Focus estimate rather than a separately reconciled customs series. Failed collection carries forward every valid component in the latest complete period as `STALE`; no prior produces `BLOCKED`.
